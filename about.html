Welcome to My Page

Technology has been a driving force in my life for as long as I can remember — not just using it, but understanding how it works from the inside out. Before I ever wrote a line of code, I was deep into things like setting up virtual machines, building computers from scratch, and modding game consoles and phones. Exploring legacy operating systems like Windows 95 and 98 gave me a real appreciation for how far computing has come.

Over the summer I took things a step further by building and hosting my own website — registered a domain, deployed with GitHub Pages, the whole deal. That project lit a fire under me, and freshman year I sat for the CompTIA A+ exam and passed, earning my first industry certification. I'm just getting started.

Outside the tech world, I'm a runner. I tried out for the Cross Country team this year and made it. At the Woodbridge XC meet I PR'd the 3-mile in 18:40 — and I'm training to break 18 minutes.
